Listenshelf

An audiobook player for the M4B and MP3 files you already own.

Listenshelf is an audiobook player for iPhone and iPad that plays audiobook files you bring yourself: M4B, MP3, M4A and AAC.

Import a file or a whole folder from the Files app and listen offline. Listenshelf reads the chapters in M4B files, plays at 0.5× to 3× without changing the pitch of the voice, has a sleep timer and bookmarks, and remembers your place and speed in every book.

Your first 2 books are free, with every feature. Listenshelf Unlimited, a one-time purchase (US$5.99 in the United States), unlocks as many books as you like. There is no subscription and no account. It can’t play DRM-protected audiobooks from Audible or Apple Books.

How it works

Import a file or a folder

Tap + in the library. Import Files… makes each file its own book. Import Folder as One Book… puts every audio file in a folder into one book, sorted by name so that Part 2 comes before Part 10. iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive work when they are set up in the Files app. You can also open an M4B from the Files app with Listenshelf.

Listening

M4B chapters appear with their titles; a book made of several files without chapters gets one chapter per file. Speed goes from 0.5× to 3× and is kept for each book. The skip buttons can be set to 10, 15, 30, 45 or 60 seconds. The sleep timer runs for 5 to 90 minutes or to the end of the chapter and fades out over the last 10 seconds. After a pause, playback goes back 3 seconds, or 10 seconds after a pause of more than 5 minutes; you can turn this off.

Bookmarks, and your files

A bookmark saves the spot, with a note if you want one. All bookmarks and notes of a book export as plain text through the share sheet. Imported books are ordinary files in the Files app under On My iPhone › Listenshelf › Audiobooks, so you can copy them out at any time.

What Listenshelf doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t play DRM-protected audiobooks, such as those from Audible or Apple Books.
  • It doesn’t sell, stream or download audiobooks. You bring the files.
  • There is no CarPlay app and no Apple Watch app.
  • Listening positions aren’t synced between an iPhone and an iPad. Each device has its own library.
  • It doesn’t unpack ZIP files. Tap the ZIP in the Files app first and it becomes a folder.
  • It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. The app is in English and German.

Questions

How do I play M4B audiobooks on my iPhone?

Use an audiobook player that reads M4B files, such as Listenshelf. Save the M4B in the Files app, then in Listenshelf tap +, choose Import Files… and pick the file. Listenshelf reads the chapters, cover, title and author inside the file and saves your place as you listen.

Is there an audiobook player for iPhone without a subscription?

Yes. Listenshelf is free for your first 2 books, with every feature and no time limit, and one purchase unlocks as many books as you like. There’s no subscription, no ads and no account, and the purchase works with Family Sharing.

How do I listen to LibriVox audiobooks on my iPhone?

Download the book from LibriVox as an M4B file if there is one, otherwise as MP3 files, and import it into Listenshelf. An M4B becomes one book with its chapters. For MP3s, keep the files in one folder and choose Import Folder as One Book…; the files are sorted by name and each one becomes a chapter. If the download is a ZIP, tap it in the Files app first to unpack it.

Can Listenshelf play my Audible or Apple Books audiobooks?

No. Audiobooks from Audible and Apple Books are protected with DRM, a copy protection that ties them to the seller’s own apps and devices, and Listenshelf can’t remove it. Listenshelf plays DRM-free files: LibriVox recordings, audiobooks sold without DRM, and CDs you ripped yourself.

Which audio formats does Listenshelf support?

M4B, M4A, MP3 and AAC. Chapters inside M4B files are shown with their titles. For MP3 files Listenshelf reads the title, album, artist and embedded cover, and uses a cover.jpg or folder.jpg in the same folder as the cover.

How do I turn a folder of MP3 files into one audiobook?

In Listenshelf, tap +, choose Import Folder as One Book… and pick the folder. Every audio file in the folder and its subfolders goes into one book, sorted by name so that Part 2 comes before Part 10, with no limit on the number of files. If the order is wrong, touch and hold the book, choose Details and File Order, and drag the files into place.

Will I lose my place in a book?

No. Listenshelf saves your position every 5 seconds, and right away when you pause, skip or leave the app; when we force-quit it in testing, about 3 seconds were lost. Every book keeps its own position and speed. Positions stay on the device and aren’t synced between an iPhone and an iPad.

How is Listenshelf different from subscription audiobook players?

Mostly in how you pay and where your books live, less in playback features. Listenshelf is one purchase after 2 free books, with no subscription and no ads, and after that it doesn’t ask for money again. Your books stay ordinary files in the Files app, and bookmarks and notes export as plain text, so nothing is locked inside the app.

Does Listenshelf need an account or an internet connection?

No. There’s no account, and playback works offline. Listenshelf collects no data; your books, positions and bookmarks stay on your device. You only need a connection to import files from a cloud service and to make the App Store purchase.

Does Listenshelf work on iPad, CarPlay or Apple Watch?

On iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, yes; on iPad the player shows chapters and bookmarks beside the cover. There is no CarPlay app and no Apple Watch app. The lock screen, Control Center, AirPods and headphone buttons work.

What does Listenshelf cost?

Your first 2 books are free, with every feature and no time limit. Listenshelf Unlimited is a one-time purchase (US$5.99 in the United States; prices vary by country) that unlocks as many books as you like and works with Family Sharing. Deleting a book doesn’t give back a free one, importing the same book again is free, and books you’ve imported keep playing either way.

Figures on this page (saving every 5 seconds, about 3 seconds lost after a force quit, the 10-second fade) come from the app’s code and our tests in the iOS Simulator, November 2026.
