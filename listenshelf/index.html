Listenshelf

An audiobook player for iPhone and iPad, for the M4B and MP3 files you already own.

Listenshelf plays audiobooks you bring yourself: DRM-free purchases, LibriVox recordings, CDs you ripped. Import a file or a whole folder from the Files app and listen offline. Your place is saved for every book.

Your first 2 books are free

Every feature, no time limits, no ads. A one-time purchase unlocks as many books as you like and is shared with your family. There is no subscription and no account.

What it does

  • Reads the chapters inside M4B files, with titles
  • A folder of MP3s becomes one book, in the right order (Part 2 before Part 10); drag files to reorder
  • Speed from 0.5× to 3×, remembered per book
  • Skip back and forward 10, 15, 30, 45 or 60 seconds
  • Sleep timer, including “end of chapter”, with a gentle fade
  • Bookmarks with notes, exported as plain text
  • Lock screen and Control Center controls, AirPods and headphone buttons, Dark Mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver

Your books stay yours

Imported books sit in the Files app under Listenshelf › Audiobooks, so you can copy them out at any time. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Supported formats: M4B, M4A, MP3, AAC. Listenshelf does not sell or stream audiobooks and cannot play DRM-protected files from Audible or Apple Books.
