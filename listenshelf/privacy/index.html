Privacy Policy for Listenshelf

In short: Listenshelf collects no data. Your audiobooks, listening positions, bookmarks and notes stay on your device.

On your device

Listenshelf copies the audio files you import into its own folder on your iPhone or iPad and reads their titles, chapters and covers there. Listening positions, bookmarks and notes are stored in the same place. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

No internet connection

The app does not connect to the internet to play, import or organise books. It contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

No account

You do not need an account. There is no subscription.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold and processed by Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies; we receive no personal data about you. The app only asks the App Store whether the unlock has been bought.

Files you export

When you export bookmarks as text, the text goes only where you choose in the share sheet.
