Tickrate collects no data

Its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”, and that is the whole policy: the developer receives nothing from the app. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting service, and the app contains no third-party code. If you turned on Share With App Developers in your device’s Analytics settings, Apple passes crash reports and usage statistics to app developers, this one included; they contain no watch data.

The microphone

Tickrate uses the microphone for two things, and only while its screen is open:

  • Listening to a watch. The sound of the ticking is analysed on your iPhone to work out how fast or slow the watch runs. The sound itself is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere; only the result (for example “gaining 2 to 5 seconds a day”) is kept.
  • Calibrating this iPhone. The app counts the microphone’s samples to time your iPhone’s clock. Nothing is heard, recorded or kept apart from the timing.

Leaving the app stops the microphone straight away.

What the app keeps, and where

Your watches, your atomic time checks, your listening results and your iPhone’s clock calibration are kept on this iPhone only. Version 1.0 has no iCloud sync and no server of its own. If you export a backup or a spreadsheet, the file goes where you choose to save or share it.

Network

The app talks to two kinds of service:

  • Public time servers, to read atomic time when you check your watch and while you calibrate your iPhone: Apple’s (time.apple.com) first, and only when it does not answer, Cloudflare’s (time.cloudflare.com) or Google’s (time.google.com). Each request carries only a timestamp. Like any connection, it comes from your network address; the company that runs the server (Apple, Cloudflare or Google) operates it.
  • The App Store, for the price of Tickrate Unlimited and your purchase.

Purchases

Apple handles the purchase of Tickrate Unlimited. The app learns only whether it is unlocked on your Apple Account.

Email

If you write to the developer, your address and message are used to answer you, and kept only as long as needed for that.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Everything it kept is on this iPhone and goes with it. Emails you sent: ask by email and they are deleted.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

Any change to this policy appears on this page with a new date.
