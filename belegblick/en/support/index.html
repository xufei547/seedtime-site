Belegblick – Support

Belegblick shows electronic invoices as a clear document you can read, print and save as PDF.

Which files can I open?

  • XRechnung (XML) in the UBL and CII syntaxes
  • ZUGFeRD 2.0 and later, and Factur-X (PDF with embedded XML data)
  • Peppol invoices and credit notes in UBL

ZUGFeRD 1.0 (the 2014 format) is not shown; the app tells you when you open such a file.

How do I open an invoice?

  • Mac: double-click, use Open With → Belegblick in Finder, drop the file on the app icon or a window, or choose File → Open.
  • iPhone and iPad: in the Files app, tap the file and pick Belegblick from the share sheet, or tap “Belegblick” in the preview of an XML file.

Does Belegblick check whether an invoice is valid?

No. Belegblick displays invoices. It does not check invoice rules and does not create invoices. Data the app can't map to a field is listed at the end under “Other data in the file”.

What does the yellow note on ZUGFeRD files mean?

A ZUGFeRD PDF contains a picture of the invoice plus the invoice data as XML. If the amount due in the XML doesn't appear on the PDF pages, Belegblick tells you. The XML data is legally binding.
