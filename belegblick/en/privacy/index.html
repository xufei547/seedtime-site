Privacy Policy for Belegblick

In short: Belegblick collects no data. Your invoices stay on your device.

Processed on your device

Belegblick reads the invoice files you open (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, Factur-X, Peppol) on your Mac, iPhone or iPad only. Their contents are never sent to us or to anyone else.

No internet connection

The app does not connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, advertising or crash reporting services and does not load anything from the web when it reads an invoice.

No account

You don't need an account. There is no subscription and there are no in-app purchases.

Files you save

When you save an invoice as PDF or save an attachment, the file is written only to the location you choose.

Purchase through the App Store

Apple handles the purchase under Apple's privacy policy. We receive no personal data about you.
