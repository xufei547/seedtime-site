Belegblick

Read e-invoices like paper. Open, check, print and save XRechnung and ZUGFeRD invoices as PDF, on Mac, iPhone and iPad.

Download on the App Store

Free to download: the first 5 invoices open free. After that a one-time purchase of €9.99 (US$8.99), no subscription. For iPhone, iPad and Mac.

What Belegblick does

  • Opens XRechnung in UBL and CII, ZUGFeRD 2.0 and later, Factur-X and Peppol invoices
  • Seller, buyer, line items, VAT, amount due and IBAN at a glance
  • For ZUGFeRD, a note when the amount due in the XML is not on the PDF pages
  • Print on A4 and save as PDF, save embedded attachments
  • Everything on your device: no internet connection, no account, no subscription

Belegblick displays invoices. It does not check invoice rules and does not create invoices.
