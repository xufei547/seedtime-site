Labelfit — Privacy Policy

Updated November 11, 2026

Deutsch · Español · Français

Labelfit collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no server. Your address lists stay on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. No account, no email address, no advertising or device identifiers, no usage statistics, no crash reporting of our own.

Your contacts and addresses

Labelfit asks for access to Contacts only when you tap Add from Contacts. It then reads names, organisations and postal addresses on your device so you can choose whom to import, and copies only the addresses you choose into your list. If you don't allow access, you can paste or type addresses instead. Labelfit never changes your contacts.

Your lists, label settings and printer corrections are stored on your device in the app's own storage. We never receive them. They leave your device only when you choose to: when you print, share or save a PDF. Your device's own iCloud backup settings apply to them as to any app.

Printing

Labels are laid out and turned into a PDF on your device. Printing goes through Apple's AirPrint to the printer you choose. Labelfit remembers that printer's address on your device so it can use the right corrections next time.

Network use

Making labels needs no network connection. The only network traffic Labelfit causes is Apple's StoreKit, used to offer the in-app purchase and to check whether you already own it, and AirPrint when you print.

Purchases

The one-time purchase Labelfit Full is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Labelfit only asks StoreKit on your device whether a purchase exists. The two free prints are counted on your device only.

Third parties

No advertising, analytics, attribution or tracking SDKs are included.

Children

Labelfit does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. Deleting the app removes the lists it stored on your device. Purchases can be restored through Apple at any time.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that sends data off your device, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the app will ask you first.
