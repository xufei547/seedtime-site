Printfit — Privacy Policy

Updated September 21, 2026

Printfit collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics. Your photos are laid out and turned into a PDF on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. No account, no email address, no advertising or device identifiers, no usage statistics, no crash reporting of our own.

Your photos

You pick photos through Apple's own photo picker, so Printfit only ever receives the images you select — it is not granted access to your library. The photos you import, and the layout you build, are stored on your device in the app's own storage. We never receive them.

Photos leave your device only when you choose to send them somewhere: when you share a PDF, open the system print dialog, or save a PDF to Files. Where it goes then is decided by you and by the service or printer you pick. Your device's own iCloud or backup settings also apply, as they do for any app.

Network use

Laying out photos and creating the PDF need no network connection and work offline. The only network traffic Printfit causes is Apple's own StoreKit, used to offer the in-app purchase and to check whether you already own it.

Purchases

The one-time purchase Printfit Full is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Printfit only asks StoreKit on your device whether an unrevoked purchase exists, and unlocks accordingly. We keep no purchase records and run no server of our own.

Third parties

No advertising, analytics, attribution or tracking SDKs are included in the app.

Children

Printfit is rated 4+ and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. Deleting the app removes the photos and layout it stored on your device. Purchases can be restored through Apple at any time.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that sends data off your device, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the app will ask you first.
