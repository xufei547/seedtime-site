Why time blindness needs a different timer

Time blindness is a common ADHD experience: time does not register as something passing, so a countdown showing 18:42 is just a fact you read, not a thing you feel. A melting object works differently. You do not have to read it. You glance, you see it is half gone, and your body understands. That is the whole idea, and it is why the melt is rendered live rather than played back as a video.

FAQ

I stepped away and my session froze — is it lost?

No. Stepping away just naps the session. Nothing withers, nothing is lost. Wake it up anytime and the timer continues where it left off.

Does the timer keep going on the Lock Screen?

Yes. Sundae uses Live Activities. Your scene and countdown stay visible on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Allow Live Activities when the app asks.

What does Sundae Full include?

Every scene, Lounge Mode, and the full collection. One $9.99 purchase, yours forever. Never a subscription. Use "Restore purchase" in Settings after reinstalling or switching phones.

Where is my data?

On your device, and only there. Sundae works completely offline, with no account, no tracking and no servers. See the privacy policy.

The ambience stopped when I locked my phone.

Background audio should keep playing. If it stops, check that Silent Mode is not on and that no other app took over audio playback.

Is the timer on this page the same as the app?

It is the same melt, ported to run in your browser. The app adds the Lock Screen Live Activity, eleven more scenes, ambient sound, Lounge Mode, the collection, and Pebble.
