FAQ

I stepped away and my session froze — is it lost?
No. Stepping away just naps the session. Nothing withers, nothing is lost. Wake it up anytime and the timer continues where it left off.

Does the timer keep going on the Lock Screen?
Yes — Sundae uses Live Activities. Your scene and countdown stay visible on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Allow Live Activities when the app asks.

What does Sundae Full include?
Every scene, Lounge Mode, and the full collection — one $9.99 purchase, yours forever. Never a subscription. Use "Restore purchase" in Settings after reinstalling or switching phones.

Where is my data?
On your device, and only there. Sundae works completely offline — no account, no tracking, no servers. See the privacy policy.

The ambience stopped when I locked my phone.
Background audio should keep playing. If it stops, check that Silent Mode isn't on and that no other app took over audio playback.
