Equaly: Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-10-26

Equaly collects no data

Its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”, and that is the whole policy: the developer receives nothing from the app. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting service, and the app contains no third-party code. If you turned on Share With App Developers in your Mac’s Analytics settings, Apple passes crash reports and usage statistics to app developers, this one included; they contain no audio.

Your Mac’s audio

To apply the equalizer to every app, Equaly needs the sound your Mac plays. macOS asks you once whether Equaly may record your system audio. Equaly uses that access only to change the sound as it plays: the audio passes through the app’s memory a few milliseconds at a time, the equalizer is applied, and it goes straight to your speakers or headphones. It is never recorded, saved to a file or sent anywhere. Equaly never uses your microphone.

What the app keeps, and where

Your equalizer settings, whether the equalizer is on, whether you have seen the welcome window, and whether the full version is unlocked are kept in the app’s own preferences on your Mac. Nothing leaves your Mac.

Network

Equaly has no network access of its own: the App Sandbox does not let it open connections. The App Store, which is part of macOS, shows the full version’s price and handles your purchase.

Purchases

Apple handles the purchase of Equaly Full. The app learns only whether Full is unlocked on your Apple Account.

Email

If you write to the developer, your address and message are used to answer you, and kept only as long as needed for that.

Deleting your data

Quit Equaly and delete it from your Applications folder. macOS keeps the app’s preferences afterwards; to remove them too, delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.xufei.eqapp. Emails you sent: ask by email and they are deleted.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

Any change to this policy appears on this page with a new date.
