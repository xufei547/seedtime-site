Equaly

A system-wide equalizer in your Mac’s menu bar

Getting started

Equaly needs macOS 14.2 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon. Open it once and it lives in your menu bar.

  1. In the welcome window, click Turn On Equalizer.
  2. macOS asks once whether Equaly may record your system audio. Choose Allow. Equaly uses this only to change the sound as it plays; it never records, saves or sends it (see the privacy policy).
  3. Play something, open Equaly from the menu bar and pick a preset. The panel shows Low latency when the equalizer is working.

Questions

I chose “Don’t Allow”, or nothing changes

Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording and turn on Equaly under “System Audio Recording Only”. If it still doesn’t work, quit Equaly from its panel and open it again. Setup & Permission… in Equaly’s panel opens the welcome window, whose Open System Settings button goes straight to that page.

What do “Low latency” and “Compatibility” mean?

On most devices Equaly adds about 8 milliseconds of delay (measured on a MacBook Air’s built-in speakers), too little to notice even in video. Now and then macOS doesn’t deliver a device’s audio to the low-latency path; Equaly notices within about two seconds and switches to Compatibility, which adds about 70 milliseconds, then tries the low-latency path again every minute and switches back when it works. You may hear a short skip when it switches.

The panel says “Waiting for audio”

That is normal until something plays. Equaly starts processing as soon as it hears sound; the first fraction of a second plays without the equalizer.

Something sounds wrong

Turn the switch at the top of the panel off: Equaly steps out of the audio path completely and your Mac sounds exactly as it did before. Quitting Equaly does the same. Presets lower the overall level a little (the preamp) so that boosted bands don’t distort; if you raise bands yourself, lower the preamp to match.

Headphones and other output devices

Equaly follows the output you choose in Control Center or System Settings › Sound. If your output is a Multi-Output Device, Equaly plays through the first device in it only while the equalizer is on. In Compatibility mode, an app that you set to play to a different device than your Mac’s output (some call apps let you choose) is heard through your Mac’s output instead.

What is free, and what does Equaly Full add?

Free: the equalizer for every app with the Flat, Bass Boost and Vocal presets. Equaly Full, a one-time purchase, adds the custom 10-band equalizer (31 Hz to 16 kHz), the preamp and all 12 presets. There is no subscription. With Family Sharing, up to five family members can use Full on their Macs.

I bought Full on another Mac

Open the panel, click Unlock, then Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Uninstalling

Quit Equaly from its panel and delete it from your Applications folder. Nothing else is installed: Equaly uses no audio driver. To remove its settings too, delete ~/Library/Containers/com.xufei.eqapp.
